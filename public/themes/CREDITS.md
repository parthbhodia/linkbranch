## Format

2:3 portrait, 1200 × 1800, WebP at quality 52 (`cobalt-grid` is 1000 × 1500 to
keep the brick detail under 200 KB). The largest file is 190 KB; the set is
about 640 KB in total and is only fetched when a photographic theme is in use.
Behind the page wash the photo reads as colour and depth rather than detail, so
there is no benefit to shipping larger files.
